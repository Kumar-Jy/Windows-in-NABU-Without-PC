## 📗 Dual Boot via Magnetic Cover

1. Reboot to **Android** if your Pad 5 is currently in Windows.
  
3. Download [**Nabu_DBKP_MagneticCover.zip**]
  - [Nabu_DBKP_MagneticCover_OldDriver.zip](https://github.com/Kumar-Jy/Windows-in-NABU-Without-PC/releases/download/Files/Nabu_DBKP_MagneticCover.zip).
    - **OR**
  - [Nabu_DBK_MagnaticCover_NewDriver.zip](https://github.com/Kumar-Jy/Windows-in-NABU-Without-PC/releases/download/Files/Nabu_DBKP_MagneticCover_NewDriver.zip).

4. Boot into **modded TWRP Recovery**.

6. Flash **`Nabu_DBKP_MagneticCover.zip`** and reboot to system when finished.

### Usage
- **Boot into Windows (from Android):** reboot your tablet and cover the screen with the magnetic cover.

- **Boot into Android (from Windows):** restart your tablet and keep the screen uncovered.

## 🔘 Dual Boot via Volume Button - ``` Deprecated for new-driver ```
1. Reboot to **Android** if your Pad 5 is currently in Windows.

3. Download [**Nabu_DBKP_VolButton.zip**](https://github.com/Kumar-Jy/Windows-in-NABU-Without-PC/releases/download/Files/Nabu_DBKP_VolButton.zip).

5. Boot into [**modded TWRP Recovery**](https://github.com/Kumar-Jy/Windows-in-NABU-Without-PC/releases/tag/Modded-TWRP-Recovery).

7. Flash **`Nabu_DBKP_VolButton.zip`** and reboot to system when finished.

### Usage
- **Boot into Windows (from Android):** reboot your tablet and press the volume button right after the Windows logo appears.  
- **Boot into Android (from Windows):** restart your tablet and wait (don’t press any button). It will automatically boot into Android.
